Research programs

Questions we are working to answer.

We connect computational methods to measured signals and careful evaluation.

01 · HEALTH

Non-invasive glucose sensing

A Raman–NIR digital-twin effort studies spectral features, calibration, and uncertainty for future non-invasive glucose monitoring.

02 · MEDIA INTEGRITY

Deepfake forensics & authenticity

We examine manipulated media, cross-generator detection, and multimodal evidence under realistic sharing conditions.

03 · RESILIENT SYSTEMS

Security for connected systems

Our work explores adversarial testing, privacy, and resilient AI in cyber-physical and sensor-rich environments.

How we work

Make the evidence—and its limits—clear.

A result matters when its assumptions, data, uncertainty, and failure cases are visible. We pair technical development with disciplined evaluation.

Measure

Ground models in meaningful signals and well-defined targets.

Challenge

Test across sources, operating conditions, and potential attacks.

Explain

Make assumptions, uncertainty, and failure modes understandable.
